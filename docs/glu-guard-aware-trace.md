# Guard-aware cone telemetry prerequisite

This component instruments the frozen isolated-GLU cone calculation. It does not implement or authorize the guard-aware gate fitting protocol. No actual calibration, candidate, target responses or evaluation seed is accessed by its synthetic controls. The scientific private-model gate stays CLOSED.

`examples/isolated_glu/guard_aware_trace.py` copies the frozen cone arithmetic while recording stages and all visited subsets, including rejected raw solutions, normalized multipliers and existing numerical KKT diagnostics. The original direction implementation remains unchanged. Finite raw solutions are stored before acceptance tests. Nonfinite solutions retain concatenated canonical little-endian float64 IEEE754 byte hex with explicit z/mu split counts instead of invalid JSON constants; they are not cleaned or used to pass KKT. Per-mask fields start empty, so failed solves cannot inherit earlier raw values. The failing geometry mask preserves its singular spectrum, ratio, exact rank and reason before abort. Global geometry failure aborts the proposal even when earlier masks had candidates. The returned direction, mask, tie rule, componentwise tolerances, conservative geometry domain and radial clipping retain their previous limited meanings. This is not a numerical completeness or exact trust-region proof.

An optional sink receives ordered proposal/subset/stage events. Callback failure aborts the attempt without retrying the observer or continuing to other subsets. TraceError retains partial in-memory evidence. Journal events record progress; complete numerical solutions reside in trace.json. Therefore an externally killed worker can leave an incomplete journal and raw problem, but is NOT guaranteed to have persisted every computed raw solution. Flushing is not fsync or power-loss durability. A truncated final journal line is reported without repair. No crash recovery or successful completion is inferred from a readable prefix.

`guard_step_evidence.py` creates an exclusive attempt directory and exclusive proposal ids, writes raw problem arrays before computation, flushes the event journal, and stores full traces and SHA256 commitments externally. Gram/constraints are held once per proposal rather than once per subset. An error in computation, journal, problem-file or final evidence writing blocks subsequent proposals/finalization retries. A filesystem failure can also prevent writing the failure manifest; propagated exceptions and incomplete files remain failures. No transactional all-files durability is claimed. After a receipt failure, any already-written summary is subordinate to the failed manifest and absent valid receipt; it cannot establish success alone.

Bounded profile: at most72 proposals, each at most256 masks and one attempted solve per mask (18,432 total subset solves); at most64 trial gate rows, dimensions1..32. The maximum successful event journal is514 lines. Receipt zero query/update counters describe this evidence helper only, not an eventual caller fit. The compact receipt links the manifest, full traces/journals/problem NPZ and numeric evidence. Trial rows and raw/weighted/residual arrays in the transport control are explicitly synthetic placeholders, not fitter output or independently validated trial decisions. This layer does not yet enforce complete fitting provenance or full accepted/final forward verification. The future fitter/controller must do so and preregister the complete schema.

The worst-size synthetic control actually computes all72x256 subset systems and carries64 trial rows and synthetic raw/weighted/residual arrays through an unchanged2KiB stdout limit. Parent controls also check old/new finite arithmetic, partial geometry failure, injected nonfinite solves, sink failure, failed attempts, truncated journals, problem-write failure and final transport/receipt failures. Strict mypy and Ruff cover the changed source. Independent source/probe review and final validation are recorded separately before treating this prerequisite as verified.
